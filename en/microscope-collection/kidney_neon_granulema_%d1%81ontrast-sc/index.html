Renal concretions in neon tetra

Microscopic preparation of neon tetra kidney. Rounded and oval structures of varying sizes with distinct boundaries and heterogeneous contents, presumably mineral concretions. Some are surrounded by cellular structures resembling a granulomatous reaction. No mycobacteria were detected, and similar lesions were not observed in other organs.
